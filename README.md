# Merveilles Pleasure Gardens

Planning repository for Merveilles Pleasure Gardens: gardens, camping,
hospitality, events, site operations, and future development.

## Planning areas

- [Gardens](docs/gardens.md) — landscape zones, paths, gathering spaces, and stewardship
- [Camping](docs/camping.md) — guest lodging concepts, capacity, amenities, and safety
- [Site operations](docs/site-operations.md) — access, parking, utilities, sanitation, and emergency planning

## Relationship to AfterFaire

Merveilles is the venue and long-term property project. AfterFaire is one
proposed event series hosted at Merveilles and remains in its own repository.

## Status

Early planning. Locations, capacities, uses, amenities, and event operations
remain conceptual until reviewed and approved where required.
