# Gardens

## Purpose

Develop Merveilles as a pleasure garden: a sequence of outdoor rooms for
walking, conversation, food, music, performance, and discovery.

## Working zones

- Garden and Grove
- Forest
- Fære
- Maravilla
- North and South Performance
- Chateau and Patio
- Studio
- Promenade and Dark Walk
- Future / conservation buffer

## Planning questions

- What character, use, and carrying capacity belongs to each zone?
- Which paths must be accessible, illuminated, or all-weather?
- Where are the quiet, performance, food-service, and habitat buffers?
- What planting and maintenance plan is realistic for Colorado conditions?
- How will fire mitigation, drainage, and guest safety shape the design?
