# Site Operations

## Core systems

- Guest arrival, credential check, parking, and departure
- All-weather emergency access
- Food preparation and service interfaces
- Potable water, sanitation, waste, and power
- Fire prevention, weather monitoring, and incident response
- Lighting, sound, neighbors, and quiet hours
- Staff, vendor, guest, and vehicle manifests

## Operating principle

Five acres create room for separation and atmosphere; they do not by themselves
establish occupancy, parking capacity, or permission for a particular use.
