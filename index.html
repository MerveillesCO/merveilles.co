I · The invitation

Merveilles · “Marvels”

The forest is not our canvas.
It is our collaborator.

Inspired by the spirit of historic pleasure gardens, Merveilles brings music, masks, mystery, and moonlight into the woods. It is not a replica of another age, but a living place for serendipity.

Follies, performances, lantern walks, games, suppers, and quiet clearings emerge slowly and deliberately. Some marvels will weather and return to earth. Others will evolve. All of them belong to the landscape.

V · Gather here

By invitation, by season

Not a park.
A stage for serendipity.

Merveilles is imagined as a home for intimate gatherings shaped by the land: small enough to feel discovered, deliberate enough to be beautifully hosted.
