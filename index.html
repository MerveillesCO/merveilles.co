I · The invitation

Merveilles · “Marvels”

A garden should
have a few secrets.

Merveilles takes its cue from the old pleasure gardens. We want music in the trees, supper outside, strange buildings, games, masks, and plenty of room to wander off.

We will make it a piece at a time. Some things will last. Others may change after a season or rot back into the ground. The woods get the final say.

V · Gather here

By invitation, by season

A place to make
and gather.

Most events here will be small and hosted. Think dinner for a long table, a concert under the trees, or a workshop that lasts all afternoon.

Nights

Music after dark

Small concerts, garden suppers, dances, readings, and lantern walks.

Days

Workshops & play

Building, gardening, rehearsing, cooking, talking, and making things together.

Stays

Camping in the glade

A few primitive campsites, by reservation and tied to hosted events.
